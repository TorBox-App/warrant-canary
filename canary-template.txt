Declarations
------------

1. We have not received a National Security Letter (NSL).
2. We have not received a FISA court order.
3. We have not received a gag order that prevents this statement.
4. We have not received a warrant for user data.
5. We have not received a subpoena for user data.
6. No agency has seized or searched our servers.
7. No third party has tampered with our infrastructure or software.
8. We have not installed a backdoor in our systems.
9. We do not log user activity beyond our published privacy policy.
10. TorBox has not been forced to sell to any external parties, and

Things we have never done:
--------------------------
1. We have never given our encryption keys or authentication keys
   to anyone. We have never given our user's private logs to anyone.
2. We have never installed law enforcement software or hardware
   on our network.
3. We have never given a law enforcement organization a live feed
   of user traffic or user content on our network.
4. We have never modified user content at the request of law
   enforcement or another third party.
5. We have never redirected a download, API response, or DNS
   response at the request of law enforcement or another third party.
6. We have never weakened or subverted our encryption at the
   request of law enforcement or another third party.

If we receive a request to do one of these things, we will use all
legal remedies to oppose it. We believe such requests are illegal.
We will fight them to protect our users.

If ever forced to do any of these things, we will exhaust all legal
remedies to protect our users from illegal requests.

Warning conditions
------------------
Do not trust TorBox if one of these conditions occurs:

- We do not publish a new canary by the next update date.
- A declaration above is removed or changed without explanation.
- The PGP signature is missing or does not validate.

If a warning condition occurs, assume that TorBox is under a legal
order that we cannot disclose.
